About

The Market Design Lab (MDL) is a research lab of the Yonsei Economic Research Institute. We conduct theoretical research in market design based on matching theory, auctions and mechanism design, information economics, game theory, and network economics, and we apply the results to the design of institutions and policies.

We connect basic research, implementation in practice, and feedback to theory in a single cycle. Our goal is to produce research that is both academically rigorous and practically useful. More about the lab

Research Areas

  • Matching Theory
  • Auction Theory & Mechanism Design
  • Information Economics
  • Game Theory & Network Economics
  • Empirical and Policy Applications
