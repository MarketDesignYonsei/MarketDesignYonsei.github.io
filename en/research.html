Research

Research Areas

Based on the fields of our faculty members, our research covers the following areas.

  • Matching TheoryResource allocation problems in which monetary transfers are restricted, such as school assignment, labor market matching, and organ transplantation
  • Auction Theory & Mechanism DesignDesign of optimal allocation and pricing mechanisms under asymmetric information
  • Information EconomicsDesign of contracts and institutions under asymmetric information
  • Game Theory & Network EconomicsResource allocation and institutional design that account for strategic interaction and network structure
  • Empirical and Policy ApplicationsEmpirical analysis and policy recommendations on current issues in Korea, including labor markets, childcare and school assignment, allocation of public resources, industrial organization, and competition policy

As the lab's activities expand, we will refine and extend these areas in line with the research of our members and the needs of Korean society.

The Market Design Lab is a research lab of the Yonsei Economic Research Institute.
